(from site, Configurator page) **Shipping enterprise AI is broken.**

- **Pilots stall out.** Teams build impressive demos that never reach production,
  because nobody planned the governance, data and integration work.
- **Every workflow is bespoke.** Prompt logic, guardrails and audit trails get
  rewritten for each use case, so nothing compounds across teams.
- **Business users wait on engineering.** The operators who know the process best
  can't change a step without a ticket and a sprint.
