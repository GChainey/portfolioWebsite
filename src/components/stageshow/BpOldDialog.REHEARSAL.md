# Rehearsal: BpOldDialog (the before)

Gold: `close-up` structure for the dialog; the framing borrows a modal over its product: the
appointment book (filler under Fluent's 40% scrim) fills the stage and runs off every edge, the
dialog floats over it as a real modal does. That keeps the whole dialog in view, which is the point.
(First tried whole on the grey: film flagged `uneven gutters`, fairly.)

Beats: Add to waiting list on (priority and date open) → Repeat appointment on (its schedule, a
conflict warning, the dates) → Urgent on (with a repeat: a combination that makes no sense) → hold
2.5s → Cancel (everything off, the loop's start). The camera eases straight to where the dialog will
end up (its open height is known ahead: the closed dialog plus each section, measured once), so it
starts comfortable (480 wide, ~0.9x) and ends small and long (~0.55x). Poster = all three on.

Cursor: hand on the switches, the priority segments, pickers and Cancel; I-beam on text fields.
Sampled every 30ms: all four presses on the hand.

Compact: a taller stage (358 × 640), the dialog drawn at 400 wide. Filmed: `--gate` at 720 and 390,
rehearsal passed. Candidates: `small text` at 390 at the longest (7–9px: the point is how small it
gets), and layout checks deferred while it grows.

Not checked: dark theme, real devices and Safari, the case-study page itself.
