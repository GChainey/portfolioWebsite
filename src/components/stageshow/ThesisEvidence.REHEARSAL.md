# ThesisEvidence: rehearsal

Homepage card for Thesis (products row), 512 × 320, filled, plays once in view.

- **Gold copied:** the RfpLetter scene's pattern (filled window, edge to edge in the card's frame); motion borrowed from `ai-cards` (a card drafted in the panel, acted on from its own button).
- **Source:** the Thesis repo's evidence and test agents (`src/lib/declaration-evidence-agent-mock.ts`, `declaration-test-agent-mock.ts`): Dovetail and Amplitude checked against a claim; strong evidence or none, then a drafted test.
- **Beats:** Check evidence on claim one → both sources searched → both found → Supported, underline turns green. Pick claim two → Check evidence → nothing in either → a drafted test opens → Start test → Running, underline turns blue. Poster = that payoff.
- **Checked:** filmed at 1 fps on the homepage card at 1440 wide; reduced-motion poster; the test card clears the panel's foot by 8px.
- **Not checked:** no compact design (the phone column shows the wide scene scaled to ~0.67, as the RFP card does); `film.mjs --gate` not run in this repo.
- **Logos:** Dovetail and Amplitude are plain wordmarks; neither mark is in the kit and a letter-in-a-square would be an invented logo.
